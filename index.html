01 / Research

Closing the loop on physical intelligence.

My work spans the algorithms, sensing, control, and hardware required for humanoid robots to operate in complex real-world environments.

R / 01

Humanoid Locomotion

Dynamic locomotion, lower-body control, whole-body coordination, and robust control for humanoid platforms.

R / 02

Planning & Optimization

Footstep planning, motion planning, constrained optimization, and Graphs of Convex Sets.

R / 03

Vision-based Control

Visual perception and environment understanding connected directly to humanoid planning and control.

R / 04

Dexterous Manipulation

Humanoid hands, fingertip sensing, grasping, manipulation, and whole-body loco-manipulation.

R / 05

Robot Learning

Reinforcement learning and data-driven policies integrated with model-based robot control.

R / 06

Humanoid Hardware

Actuator development, embedded systems, sensing, and end-to-end hardware/software integration.

Current direction

A hierarchical, hybrid robot stack

Model-based robotics and optimization provide structure; perception and learning provide adaptation. The goal is a system that carries intent all the way to reliable physical behavior.

Structure Models + Optimization
Adaptation Perception + Learning
Output Physical humanoid behavior

Planning & Optimization

Humanoid Footstep Planning using Graphs of Convex Sets

Optimization-based humanoid footstep planning using Graphs of Convex Sets, targeting high-quality plans without requiring an initial discrete path or initial continuous foot-placement values.

04 / About

From algorithms to experiments.

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Mechanical Engineering at KAIST and a member of the Dynamic Robot Control & Design Lab. My research focuses on humanoid robotics, particularly the connection between motion planning, whole-body control, perception, learning, and physical robot systems.

I am interested in research that closes the loop from algorithmic ideas to real robot experiments.
