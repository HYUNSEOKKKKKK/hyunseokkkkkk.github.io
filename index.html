I am a Ph.D. candidate in Mechanical Engineering at KAIST, advised by Prof. Hae-Won Park in the Dynamic Robot Control & Design Lab. I work on humanoid locomotion, motion planning, manipulation, and robot hardware.

Previously, I was a visiting researcher at the German Aerospace Center (DLR), working on footstep planning for TORO. I received my B.S. and M.S. in Mechanical Engineering from KAIST in 2023 and 2025.

I connect perception, planning, and control on real robots.

Hopper: Hardware to Learned Control

Hardware & control

A single-leg robot developed from mechanism and onboard electronics to state estimation and learned standing and hopping control. A parallel ankle connects hardware design with real-world policy deployment.

My work: hardware, onboard integration, simulation, and control.

PiPER: RGB-D Perception to Manipulation

Perception & manipulation

Estimating cuboid SE(3) poses from a wrist-mounted RGB-D camera and using them for grasp planning and real-arm pick-and-place. One recorded run transfers four hand-fed boxes after a single initial approval.

KAIST Humanoid

DRCD Lab · Collaborative project

Our lab’s full-size humanoid platform combines in-house actuation with learning-based locomotion and whole-body interaction. The official v0.7 video shows field tests and interaction demonstrations.

Adaptive Magnetic Foot for MARVEL

Magnetic adhesion & mechanism design

A magnetic foot combining multiple miniaturized electro-permanent magnets with a differential ankle mechanism, designed to maintain distributed contact on curved, stepped, and protruded ferromagnetic surfaces.
