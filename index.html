01 / Research

Closing the loop on physical intelligence.

My work spans the algorithms, sensing, control, and hardware required for humanoid robots to operate in complex real-world environments.

R / 01

Humanoid Locomotion

Dynamic locomotion, lower-body control, whole-body coordination, and robust control for humanoid platforms.

R / 02

Planning & Optimization

Footstep planning, motion planning, constrained optimization, and Graphs of Convex Sets.

R / 03

Vision-based Control

Visual perception and environment understanding connected directly to humanoid planning and control.

R / 04

Dexterous Manipulation

Humanoid hands, fingertip sensing, grasping, manipulation, and whole-body loco-manipulation.

R / 05

Robot Learning

Reinforcement learning and data-driven policies integrated with model-based robot control.

R / 06

Humanoid Hardware

Actuator development, embedded systems, sensing, and end-to-end hardware/software integration.

Current research system

Perception to planning to control

02 / Selected Projects

Research built as working systems.

Selected projects grounded in active implementations across optimization, perception, learning, and humanoid control.

Featured / Planning & Optimization

Humanoid Footstep Planning using Graphs of Convex Sets

A discrete-continuous planning pipeline for humanoid footsteps using Graphs of Convex Sets, with implementations for terrain generation, relaxed-to-fixed optimization, and receding-horizon replanning.

  • GCS
  • Sliding-window replanning
  • Python + C++
02

RGB-D Perception / Sim-to-Real

Foundation-model Foothold Perception

A perception stack for selecting steppable footholds from onboard RGB-D sensing, using frozen visual and depth features with learned fusion across simulation and real-camera workflows.

  • RGB-D
  • Feature fusion
  • Foothold selection
03

Control / Reinforcement Learning

Learning-based Footstep Tracking

A RaiSim-based PPO controller for tracking commanded foot positions and yaw while balancing contact, smoothness, body orientation, and constraint-related objectives.

  • PPO
  • RaiSim
  • Foot tracking
04

Humanoid Learning Stack

Depth-aware Humanoid Locomotion

A recurrent policy stack combining proprioception and onboard depth observations, supported by reusable humanoid, biped, and hopping-leg simulation environments.

  • Depth encoder
  • Recurrent policy
  • Humanoid simulation
05

Generative Models / Planning Studies

Terrain Generation & Planning Benchmarks

Conditional stepping-stone generation paired with comparative footstep-planning implementations spanning GCS, mixed-integer optimization, and A* graph search.

  • CVAE
  • MIQCQP
  • A*

04 / About

From algorithms to experiments.

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Mechanical Engineering at KAIST and a member of the Dynamic Robot Control & Design Lab. My research focuses on humanoid robotics, particularly the connection between motion planning, whole-body control, perception, learning, and physical robot systems.

I am interested in research that closes the loop from algorithmic ideas to real robot experiments.
